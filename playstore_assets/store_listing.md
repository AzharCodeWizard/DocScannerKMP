## 2. Full Description (formatted for Google Play Store)

Turn your phone into a powerful, portable AI-powered document scanner and PDF studio! **DocScanner** is designed for students, professionals, accountants, and businesses who demand crisp, clear, high-resolution scans with 100% on-device privacy.

Whether you need to scan multi-page contracts, book pages, receipts, ID cards, business cards, or notes, DocScanner delivers crystal-clear results in seconds with automated edge detection, perspective dewarping, and advanced GPU color enhancement presets.

---

### 🌟 KEY FEATURES

#### 📷 1. Smart Camera Scanner with AI Edge Detection
- **Real-Time Document Border Detection**: Automatically identifies document boundaries and contours.
- **Perspective Dewarping**: Fixes skewed angles and shadows with 8-point precision corner adjustment.
- **Rule-of-Thirds & Spirit Level**: Capture perfectly aligned pages every single time.
- **Batch Scanning Mode**: Scan multi-page documents, contracts, and books continuously.

#### 🎨 2. GPU-Powered Color & Filter Enhancements
- **Magic Color & Magic Color 2**: Restores vivid colors while brightening yellowed or dark backgrounds.
- **B&W & Grayscale Modes**: Perfect for black-and-white printouts, receipts, invoices, and forms.
- **Fine-Tuning Controls**: Real-time sliders for Contrast, Brightness, Saturation, and Sharpness.
- **Hold to Compare**: Instant side-by-side preview with original captured photo.

#### 🆔 3. 2-in-1 ID Card & Passport Scanner
- Scan front and back of driver’s licenses, identity cards, or badges.
- Automatically stitches both sides onto a single standard A4 page ready for official submission.

#### 🔍 4. Fast On-Device OCR (Optical Character Recognition)
- Extract text instantly from scanned pages, receipts, and images.
- 100% On-Device Processing via Google ML Kit — your sensitive text never leaves your phone.
- Copy, search, edit, and export extracted text blocks with one tap.

#### 🔒 5. Professional PDF Tools & Security
- **AES-256 Password Encryption**: Protect confidential documents with secure passwords.
- **Custom Watermarking**: Prevent unauthorized sharing with anti-leak watermarks.
- **Electronic Signatures (E-Sign)**: Draw and place your signature directly onto documents.
- **PDF Compression**: Choose High Quality, Balanced, or Low Size for email attachments.
- **Page Formats**: Export in A4, US Letter, or US Legal standards.

#### 📱 6. QR & Barcode Studio
- **Google Pay Style Auto-Zooming Scanner**: Detects and zooms into far-away QR codes automatically.
- **Multi-Type QR Generator**: Create custom QR codes for URLs, Wi-Fi networks, UPI payments, Contact Cards (vCard), and plain text.
- **Save & Share Vault**: Organize scanned and generated QR codes in your personal vault.

#### 📂 7. Document Organization & Tagging
- Organize documents into custom folders: Receipts, Invoices, ID Cards, Legal, Personal.
- Full-text search by title, tags, and OCR recognized text.
- Grid and List view options with customizable sorting.

#### 🛡️ 8. Privacy-First Architecture
- **100% Offline Capability**: Scans and images are stored locally in your device's private storage.
- No mandatory accounts, no watermarks forced on free tiers, and zero data selling.

## 5. Required App Permissions Explanation
- `CAMERA`: Required to capture photos of documents, ID cards, and scan QR codes.
- `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`: Required to import existing photos and documents from gallery.
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: (Optional) For background PDF export completion alerts.
